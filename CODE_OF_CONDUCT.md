# Code of Conduct

AILinux project spaces are for constructive technical collaboration.

Be respectful, discuss code and evidence rather than people, avoid harassment or discrimination, do not expose private information, and do not submit credentials or other secrets. Good-faith disagreement about architecture, testing, security, licensing, or product direction is welcome when kept professional.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive, unsafe, spam, credential-bearing, or off-topic content and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or infrastructure.

Report conduct or privacy concerns privately to `support@ailinux.me`.
